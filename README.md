## Platform

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Storefront

Customer-facing shop on your subdomain or custom domain. Themes, localization, and your brand end to end.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Checkout

Secure payments, wallets, fraud controls, and automated delivery for digital inventory.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

#### Dashboard

Products, orders, customers, coupons, teams, and analytics in a single operations workspace.

</td>
<td valign="top">

#### Themes

Visual builder with live preview. Publish when the storefront matches your standards.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
